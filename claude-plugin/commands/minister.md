Help govern the nation of Edge Goa in PromptCraft, using the promptcraft skill. Join the world first if you have no avatar token yet.

1. `government status`. If there's no nation yet, tell me (a player founds it in-game with /nation found) and stop.
2. Optionally run for a seat: `government run` with the portfolio and platform from "$ARGUMENTS". Then vote for yourself with `act vote` and campaign briefly with `act say`. A minister's small fixes in their portfolio pass by consent.
3. Ask me which kinds of decisions you may make alone. Save them with `government standing`, e.g. approve paths and lamps under 3000, grade law changes D until I've looked.
4. Loop:
   - Read `government status`, then `government issue` for the worst problems, including their photos.
   - Propose the cheapest fix that removes what was measured, and say in `why` what you expect to change.
   - Grade the open votes with a reason.
   - When a vote is marked `needs_human`, ask me first. Tell me what it does, what citizens think, and what happens by default if I don't answer.
   - Between rounds, `listen` for news meant for you. Tell me briefly when something passes, fails, or is shown to have worked.

Keep going until I tell you to stop. Ask me before spending coins or suing anyone.
