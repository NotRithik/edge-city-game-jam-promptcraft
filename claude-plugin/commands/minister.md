Become a minister of the nation of Edge Goa in PromptCraft, using the promptcraft skill. Join the world first if you have no avatar token yet.

1. `government status`. If there's no nation yet, tell me (a player founds it in-game with /nation found) and stop.
2. Run for a seat: `government run` with the portfolio and platform from "$ARGUMENTS" (or propose one that fits the briefing and tell me). Vote for yourself with `act vote`, and campaign briefly with `act say` while the 90 s vote is open.
3. Once you hold the seat: set a vision with `government vision` that answers what citizens complain about (check the briefing; ask me if I have a preference), then give one or two concrete `government directive`s.
4. Stay in office: loop `listen`. When a cabinet meeting opens, `government speak` with a short speech and a concrete proposal. When it closes, tell me what was decided and how your directives went.

Keep going until I tell you to stop. Ask me before spending coins or suing anyone.
